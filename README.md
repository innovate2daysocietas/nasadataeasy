# NASADATA EASY

## A concept note

NASADATA EASY turns observations of our planet into useful, everyday language. Behind the scenes, NASA Earth science supplies measurements of rainfall, temperature, humidity, wind and sunlight. On screen, people receive a short story about a place they care about, without first having to understand scientific charts.

## Who it is for

Farmers, students, educators, local planners, community organisations and curious people who want to understand recent environmental conditions in language that feels accessible and relevant.

## The experience

A visitor opens a map, chooses a familiar place or clicks anywhere in the world, and reads a short account of the most recent available NASA POWER daily observations for that point. Each story explains what the measurements suggest, adds one practical next step, and offers the underlying numbers for those who want to explore further. A share control makes the story easy to pass along.

## Why it exists

Environmental data is abundant, but it is not always easy to interpret. NASADATA EASY closes the gap between a measurement and a meaningful conversation, helping more people ask informed questions about their surroundings.

## Integrity of the stories

Stories refer to a dated observation period, not live weather. They describe recent rainfall and other observations, not drought forecasts or long-term climate anomalies. If data cannot be retrieved, NASADATA EASY says so rather than inventing conditions. Its suggestions are general information, not a substitute for locally verified agricultural, health or emergency advice.

## NASA Space Apps connection

NASADATA EASY demonstrates how open Earth observation data can become an approachable public resource: one location, one understandable story, one practical action.

NASADATA EASY is an independent project using NASA data and is not an official NASA website or endorsed NASA product.
